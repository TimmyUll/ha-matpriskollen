# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- Products entered during setup are checked against this week's store pages, with suggestions for misspelt names. The pages read during setup are reused, so they are not read twice.
- Fixed a setup error when a product was added or removed while the stores were being read for the first time.

## 0.3.1
- *3 för 2* next to a price per item counts as two thirds of that price.

## 0.3.0
- A new product is checked against this week's store pages: number of offers and the cheapest, or similar names when it is not found.

## 0.2.0
- Setup asks for the first products after the stores.

## 0.1.0
- First version: choose stores by town or link, follow products with a price limit and notifications.
